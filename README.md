## Know the exam and the role it assesses

The Fortinet NSE 5 - SD-WAN 7.6 Core Administrator exam evaluates applied Secure SD-WAN deployment, configuration, administration, and operation. It is intended for network and security professionals handling integration, operational scenarios, logs, and troubleshooting.

The verified examination information includes:

- Time allowed: 60–70 minutes.
- Length: 30–35 questions.
- Scoring: pass or fail.
- Language: English.
- Product versions: FortiOS 7.6 and FortiManager 7.6.
- A score report available through the Pearson account.

Fortinet's experience guidance lists three years in networking, three years in network security, and two years with FortiGate and FortiManager. Treat these as separate experience categories rather than adding them into an eight-year requirement.

## Keep the complete official scope in view

SD-WAN Architecture and Concept has a 15–25% range. It includes enterprise architecture, DIA topologies and setup, members and zones, underlay and overlay links, and health checks. Review Performance SLAs, monitoring methods, targets, member status, and advanced settings.

SD-WAN Traffic Control, Rules and Routing has a 35–45% range. Study rule objectives and strategies, matching criteria, local-out and implicit rules, member selection, balancing, quality metrics, SLA maps, and minimum-SLA-meet-members. Routing and session controls are included alongside those rule subjects.

Centralized Management, visibility and troubleshooting also has a 35–45% range. It covers FortiManager, templates, settings, metadata variables, groups, widgets, traffic logs, events, and troubleshooting output.

These are official ranges, not fixed allocations. Do not replace them with midpoint percentages or treat them as a promise of exact question counts.
